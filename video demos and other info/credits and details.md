Fun fact: while most mammals are generally considered to have bilaterally symmetrical skulls, horse skulls naturally exhibit some degree of asymmetry. This is due to both minor, random developmental variations (fluctuating asymmetry) and a consistent, species-wide directional asymmetry, particularly in the nasal and palatine regions.
